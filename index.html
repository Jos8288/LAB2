About Me

My name is Josefine Söderholm, and I'm originally from Stockholm, Sweden. I have a background in project management, but my interest in web development started through my older sister, who works as a frontend developer. Seeing what she created sparked my curiosity about programming and inspired me to explore how technology, design, and creativity can work together.

That curiosity led me to study the Webmaster Program at University West, where I developed skills in frontend and backend development as well as UX/UI design. I especially enjoy the combination of solving technical problems and creating digital experiences that are both functional and user-friendly.

One of my strongest childhood memories is spending summer days by the sea. I have always been drawn to the ocean, sunshine, and warm weather, and that connection has stayed with me into adulthood, eventually becoming a passion for surfing and traveling. I have always wished summer could last a little longer. When the maple leaves begin to change color, I know my favorite season is coming to an end.

My future goal is to build a career that gives me the freedom to work independently and remotely within web development and design. For me, that freedom means being able to combine a creative career with the lifestyle I value — traveling, spending time by the ocean, and continuing to surf throughout the year.
